Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Milescope (“we”, “us”) is an independent website at milescope.vercel.app that shows where credit card points can take you. This policy explains what information the site handles, why, who it goes to, and the choices you have. We don’t sell your information, we don’t show ads, and we don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

The short version. Without an account, what you enter stays in your own browser. If you create an account, we store your email and your saved wallet, trips and home airport so they sync across devices, and you can delete it all at any time. Questions you ask the assistant, sentences you type into “describe a trip” search, and voice input are sent to the services that process them.

1. Information we handle

Stored in your browser (not sent to us)

  • Your points balances and which cards you hold, your saved trips, and whether you’ve finished setup.
  • A seats.aero API key, if you add one in Settings.
  • If you sign in, a login session token.

This stays on your device in your browser’s local storage. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

If you create an account

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our account provider). If you use “Continue with Google”, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Saved data: your points balances, which cards you hold, your saved trips and your home airport.

We never ask for card numbers, bank logins or loyalty program passwords, and you should never enter them.

When you use certain features

  • Miles, the assistant: your question, the recent conversation, and a summary of your balances and the trips from your home airport are sent to an AI provider to generate an answer.
  • Describe-a-trip search: the sentence you type is sent to an AI provider that turns it into search filters.
  • Voice input: uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition. Your browser maker may process the audio on its own servers (for example Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari) under its own privacy policy. The microphone level that animates the glow is measured on your device and not sent anywhere.
  • Live seat check: the route and cabin you check, plus your seats.aero key if you added one, are sent through our server to seats.aero.

Collected automatically

  • Usage analytics: we use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views and a few in-app events (like opening the assistant or checking seats) without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider keeps standard request logs (such as IP address, browser type and time) for security and reliability. We use IP addresses briefly to limit abuse of the assistant.

2. How we use it

  • To show routes, prices and transfer plans for your wallet.
  • To save and sync your data when you have an account.
  • To answer assistant questions and interpret trip descriptions.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent abuse, fix problems and understand which features are used.

We don’t sell or rent your personal information, we don’t use it for advertising, and we don’t make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that run the site, to the extent needed for each feature:

VercelHosts the website and its server functions; provides cookieless analytics.

Google“Continue with Google” sign-in (if you use it). Also serves the site’s fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users or others, or as part of a transfer of the project, in which case this policy would continue to apply.

4. Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we use your Google name, email address and profile picture only to create and identify your Milescope account. We don’t use Google user data for advertising, don’t sell it, and don’t transfer it except to the providers above as needed to run your account. Milescope’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove Milescope’s access at any time in your Google Account settings.

5. How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. When you do, your account and saved data are deleted from our database right away (backups kept by our provider roll off on their normal schedule). Assistant and search requests aren’t stored by us; the AI providers handle them under their own retention policies. Server logs are kept for a limited period by our hosting provider.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Use it without an account. Accounts are optional.
  • Delete your account at any time: sign in, open Account, and choose “Delete my account”.
  • Access, correct or get a copy of your data: your saved data is shown in the app, and you can edit it there. For a copy, email us.
  • Clear local data by clearing your browser’s site data for milescope.vercel.app.

Depending on where you live (for example California, Virginia, Colorado or other US states, or the EU/UK), you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete or port your personal information, and to opt out of its “sale” or “sharing”. We don’t sell or share personal information for targeted advertising. To make a request, email masseyjudge@gmail.com; we’ll respond within the time the law requires and won’t treat you differently for using your rights.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Account data is protected by access rules so each user can only read and change their own records, and passwords are stored only as secure hashes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

8. Children

Milescope is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

9. International users

Milescope is operated from the United States, and our providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll give notice on the site.
