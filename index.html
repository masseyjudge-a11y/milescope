      Transfer partners, in short

      Transfer partners

      Your card points can move into airline programs, usually one to one. Once moved, you book with the airline's miles.

      Before you transfer

      Transfers can't be undone. Find a seat first, then move only what you need.

      Sweet spots

      Some programs charge far fewer miles for the same flight. Those routes are marked Sweet spot.

      Why not the travel portal?

      Portals usually give about 1¢ per point. Transferring often gets you two to three times that.

      Latest transfer & points deals

      How this works

      Pick your airport and enter your points. Milescope shows every trip you can book and the cheapest way to pay for it.

      Bright routes are ones you can book now. Tap any route for the details.

      How deals stay current

      Every 6 hours, Milescope reads the big points blogs and uses TypeSafe's Jev model to spot new transfer bonuses. A bonus is only added if Jev is confident and the card really transfers to that airline. Anything unclear is skipped. Transfer partners are checked by hand.

      About the prices

      Prices are typical award prices, updated September 2026. A "~" means the airline changes prices often. Check real seats before you move points.
